Coordinator Halvorsen's script start to finish · flat voice, over the mic

  1. Welcomemonitor 00
    “Welcome aboard, Agents. You have been selected for the Psychic Agents program. Training takes place under real conditions: eight adult specimens are confined beneath the floor. Your contract covers the risks.”
  2. The rulesmonitor 1
    “Look at the screen. Protocol set by the company. Only the Coordinator knows the sealing order.”
  3. Monitor 1start
    “Each agent chooses the room where they start the training. You may change rooms until you confirm.”
  4. Monitor 2moves
    “Before each sealing, I draw a W.Y.C. card: it sets the number of mandatory moves. One move: one adjacent room, through a door, a lift or a duct. No stopping in lifts or ducts.”
  5. Monitor 3sealing
    “I announce the sealed room. A xenomorph is released into it. Any agent inside that room is eliminated.”
  6. Monitor 4end
    “Eight successive sealings. Survivors: the agents in the last room left open. Trust your instinct, Agents. Questions: not authorized.”
  7. Starton the map
    “Please proceed to the room of your choice.” [they tap a room] “Confirm your position.” [Confirm] “Position recorded. Training begins.”
  8. Each turnW.Y.C. card
    “W.Y.C. card. Moves authorized: [number].” [they move their token] “Moves complete. Confirm.”
  9. Each turnprotocol
    “Sealing in progress: [room].” [tap the room; slam, scream] “Specimen released.” Two rooms: “Double sealing: [room] and [room].”
  10. If they objector hesitate
    “I follow company protocol.” · “Trust your instincts, Agent.” · “Next turn.”
  11. 8th sealingend
    “Final sealing.” [a beat] “Training complete. Your file has been forwarded.” [read the report; fresh sheet:] “Next agent.”
