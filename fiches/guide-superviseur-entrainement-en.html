01 · SYNOPSIS the original scenario

The player is a volunteer Psychic Agent, recruited by Weyland-Yutani for exceptional perceptive abilities and very well paid. The future mission: sensing the presence of xenomorph creatures hidden aboard the company's colony ships.

Before any mission, the agent must pass training on the Nostromo II lunar base, a replica of a mid-sized cargo ship. Training takes place under live conditions: eight adult specimens are confined beneath the floor. The Coordinator seals the rooms one by one; a xenomorph is released into each sealed room. Only the agent standing in the last room left open survives. The player knows all of this: the tension comes from instinct, not from a secret.

02 · PREPARATION before the player arrives

  • The app: Start → Sixth Sense → Launch (default choice). Check sound and brightness.
  • The Psychic Agent pre-gen sheet printed double-sided, male version (nostromo-sixth-sense-sheet-male.pdf) or female version (nostromo-sixth-sense-sheet-female.pdf); the Supervisor Aid separately (nostromo-sixth-sense-supervisor-aid.pdf).
  • The Coordinator's paper protocol sheets and the W.Y.C. cards, kept in order, out of the player's sight.
  • Four six-sided dice (3D6, plus one for advantage) and a pencil to tick off EP.

03 · STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH about 20 to 30 minutes

1

Welcome aboard — welcome monitor, briefing room B-7

The first monitor reads WELCOME ABOARD, AGENT.: selection for the Psychic Agents program, future mission, base under live conditions, eight specimens beneath the floor, risks covered by the contract. Let the player read, then hand over the sheet.

2

The 4 protocol rules — monitors 1 to 4

The instructor scrolls through monitors 1 to 4 (at the bottom: QUESTIONS: NOT AUTHORIZED) and dodges every question (p. 3). “The protocol for this training was established by the company. Only the Coordinator knows the sealing order.” Four panels: starting position (the agent may change their mind before confirming), moves (W.Y.C. card: number of required moves; no stopping in a lift or a duct), sealing (a xenomorph is released; any agent present is eliminated), end of training (eight sealings; only the last open room survives). Sign-off: “Trust your instinct, Agent.”

3

The game on the map — eight sealings

“Please proceed to the room of your choice.” The agent picks a starting room. At each step, the Coordinator draws a W.Y.C. card and announces the number of moves; the player moves their pawn; the Coordinator checks his paper protocol sheet, announces “Sealing in progress.” and taps on the map the room to seal. The app plays the creature's shriek. Between sealings, make the rolls from section 05: the player “senses” a presence or not, but the choice of where to go is theirs alone.

4

The final report — training or incident report

The app displays AGENT SURVIVED or AGENT ELIMINATED (texts p. 4). The Coordinator reads it in a neutral voice, then the Supervisor plays the epilogue.

04 · Playing the Coordinator HALVORSEN

Tall, gaunt, grey uniform. He speaks over the intercom from the control room, in a flat voice. He never looks at the agent: he looks at his sheets.

  • “Please proceed to the room of your choice.”
  • “Sealing in progress.”
  • “I follow company protocol.”
  • “Trust your instinct, Agent.”
  • He follows his paper protocol sheets, period. He improvises nothing, comments on nothing, justifies nothing.
  • He never gives a hint about the next sealing, even if the agent begs.
  • NPC: Physical 3, Mental 4, Social 2, EP 7; Discipline ■■■: nothing rattles him.

05 · 3D6 Système rolls ideas

  • Mental, Average (2), to sense a presence: a shiver, a heartbeat, a smell. The Supervisor describes a sensation, never a certainty or a safe room.
  • Meditation: Mental, Easy (1), advantage, to regain composure after a nearby sealing. Failure: −1 EP.
  • Athletics: Physical, Average (2), advantage, to chain moves, slip into a duct, avoid stumbling.
  • EP: 1–2 weakened (disadvantage), 0 the agent collapses and stays put.
  • The dice color the story; they never change the required moves or the sealings: the protocol sheet is final.

## 3D6 SYSTÈME: roll 3D6; each die ≤ the tested attribute (Physical, Mental or Social, 1 to 5) = 1 success · difficulty = successes required: Easy 1, Average 2, Hard 3, Extreme 4 · advantage = 4D6, disadvantage = 2D6 (all dice count; no stacking, one cancels the other) · triple (111…666): ≤ attribute = critical success, > attribute = critical failure · opposed test: most successes wins (tie: reroll, advantage to the player, or draw) · mastery: rank 1 = advantage, rank 2 = narrative effect (often 1 EP), rank 3 = major effect · EP = Physical + Mental; 1–2 weakened, 0 unconscious, negative = critical condition.

06 · The briefing room B-7

Setup: the app's intro screens are the wall monitors of room B-7. The Supervisor holds the device facing the player like a monitor; the instructor (the Coordinator) stands beside it and scrolls through the screens. Rules monitors 1 to 4 display at the bottom QUESTIONS: NOT AUTHORIZED.

The player sits among three other Agents in training, barely sketched “silhouettes”:

  • Pike, the nervous one — bites his nails. “What if we pick the wrong room?” — “Everything is on the screen.”
  • Brannigan, the braggart — ex-marine, “I can sense them three decks away.” “Can we see a specimen first?” — “Next question.”
  • Okoye, the silent one — says nothing, stares at monitor 3. If the player meets her eyes, she mouths: “Don't ask anything.”

Use: they ask the questions the player doesn't dare to, and get shut down; the player thus gauges the wall they are up against. During the game, their fate stays off-screen.

07 · The instructor dodges zero answers

  • “Everything is on the screen.”
  • “Next question.”
  • “That point will be covered during training.”
  • “The company has already answered.”
  • “Look at screen 3.”
  • “Your question has been noted.”
  • “That point is not covered by procedure.”
  • “Next screen.”
  • “I am not authorized to answer.”
  • “Sit down, Agent.”

Prying out a hint: Social test, difficulty Hard (3), or Extreme (4) if the question touches on the protocol. Advantage if the player draws on a useful mastery or on a silhouette who just pushed; disadvantage if already shut down or if the player cuts the instructor off.

What they get: a mood hint, never game information. For example: a brief glance at the floor, a burn scar on his hand, “Stay calm: that's all that matters.” Nothing about the sealing order or the protocol: the instructor follows his paper protocol sheets, period.

08 · ENDINGS texts displayed by the app

TRAINING REPORT · TRAINING COMPLETE.
AGENT SURVIVED

“Congratulations, Agent. You have survived every sealing in the protocol. Final position: [room]. Your Psychic Agent abilities are confirmed: you sensed every xenomorph presence aboard the base ship. The company thanks you for your commitment. Your file is being forwarded for a first mission.”

Epilogue: aptitude confirmed. The Coordinator stamps the file without a word. In the corridor, mission orders are already waiting: a colony freighter, a crew that knows nothing, and the agent as the only detector. The pay is commensurate.

INCIDENT REPORT
AGENT ELIMINATED

“Room sealed: [room]. A specimen has been released into it. You were inside. In accordance with your contract, the risks of this training were known and accepted. The company regrets the loss of its agent. A new candidate will be selected.”

Epilogue: the Coordinator files away his sheet and takes a fresh one. Over the intercom: “Next agent.” The player can restart with a new Psychic Agent (same sheet, different name).

09 · The pre-gen at a glance Agent Marek or Lena NOVAK · player sheet

Human, EDGE Sixth sense (once per session, advantage on a Mental test to sense a presence) · Physical 2 · Mental 4 · Social 2 · EP 6 · former watch operator on a mining colony.

Masteries: Investigation ■■ (occupation), Meditation ■■, Athletics ■■, Survival ■, Empathy ■, Discipline ■. FLAW: claustrophobic (disadvantage on tests made in an air duct or a lift).
