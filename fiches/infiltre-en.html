05 · Background and mission C.R.F. TRANSMISSION

In the uniform of the Psychic Agents, I play the model trainee. No one knows I answer to the C.R.F., the Civil Resistance Force.

One night, I sent everything to HQ: the W.Y.C. has not given up on taming the xenomorphs. Adult specimens are waiting beneath the floor, and their hosts are said to be death-row convicts . With the report, I attached the base map and the A.I.P. protocols: each one dictates a session's room sealing order. Which one will they use? I have no idea.

My last dose of NS-X III, the neuro-stimulant that kept me on my feet, was ago. All I have left are my nerves.

MISSION
Hold out until the extraction promised by the C.R.F. and bring back the evidence.

What the agent doesn't know SCENARIO SECRET

Management knows everything. The report to HQ was intercepted and forwarded to the Coordinator “for execution”. Rather than arrest the agent, Weyland-Yutani sends them to the training session, no exemption, NS-X III withdrawn: a punishment disguised as an exercise. The agent finds out on the intro screen.

The Coordinator runs the session as usual: he announces and touches the sealings following his paper protocol sheets. He follows company protocol, period. Dice never change the sealings.
