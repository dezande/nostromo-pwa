01 · SYNOPSIS what the player doesn't know yet

The player plays Lieutenant D.R., a C.R.F. (Civil Resistance Force) agent infiltrated into the secret Nostromo II lunar base under cover as a Psychic Agent in training. On 10/22/2184 at 23:47, he sent an encrypted report to his secret base, the C.R.F.'s Earth HQ: Weyland-Yutani is pursuing its xenomorph control project, adult specimens are confined beneath the floor, and the hosts are said to be death-row convicts. He attached the base map and a copy of the A.I.P. (Alien Intrusion Protocol) protocols: each sets the order in which rooms are sealed during a training session, without saying which one will be used. He was holding on only thanks to the neuro-stimulant NS-X III.

Management knows everything. His report was intercepted and forwarded to the Coordinator with a header: the sender will take part in the next training session, “no exemptions”, NS-X III withdrawn; no other sanction will be notified. Rather than arrest him and alert the C.R.F., the company lets the session decide. The player discovers it on the single intro screen: his own message, topped with the INTERCEPTED stamp… just before the session begins.

02 · PREPARATION before the player arrives

  • The app: Start → Disciplinary Session → Launch. Check the device's sound and brightness.
  • Lieutenant D.R.'s pre-gen sheet printed double-sided, male version (nostromo-sheet-lieutenant-dr-male.pdf) or female version (nostromo-sheet-lieutenant-dr-female.pdf); the Supervisor Aid separately (nostromo-supervisor-aid-infiltrated.pdf).
  • The Coordinator's paper protocol sheets and the W.Y.C. cards, kept in order, out of the player's sight.
  • Four six-sided dice (3D6, plus one for advantage) and a pencil to tick off EP.

03 · STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH about 30 minutes

1

The intercepted e-mail — the single intro screen

The app displays COORDINATOR'S TERMINAL · INTERCEPTED COPY. Staging of your choice: the Coordinator left his terminal on when leaving the briefing room, or he silently projects the copy on the wall monitor. At the bottom of the message: his own report of 10/22/2184, which the player recognizes. At the top: the INTERCEPTED stamp and management's header. At the foot: “Forwarded to the Coordinator for execution.” Full text in the appendix (p. 4).

2

The dramatic core — he has been found out

Let the player read his message first, then scroll up to the header. Say nothing. The moment he realizes management knows everything, and that his NS-X III has been withdrawn, is the scenario's peak: the instructor stays stone-faced, puts away his sheets, then moves on to the rules monitors. Possible roll: Mental to keep his composure while reading (p. 2).

3

The rules of the “disciplinary session” — monitors 1 to 4, room B-7

The instructor scrolls through the wall monitors (at the bottom: QUESTIONS: NOT AUTHORIZED) and dodges every question (p. 3). Header DISCIPLINARY SESSION · ALIEN INTRUSION PROTOCOL: “Session ordered by the Security Directorate.” The four panels are those of the training: starting position, moves (W.Y.C. card), sealing (a xenomorph is introduced into the sealed room), end of session (eight sealings; only those in the last open room survive). Signature: “Trust your instincts, Agents.”

4

The game on the map — eight sealings

“Please proceed to the room of your choice.” D.R. picks his starting room. At each step, the Coordinator draws a W.Y.C. card and announces the number of moves; the player moves his pawn; the Coordinator checks his paper protocol sheet, announces “Sealing in progress.” and taps on the map the room to seal. He doesn't comment, justify or improvise anything.

5

End — Security Directorate report

The app displays THE INFILTRATOR SURVIVED (with a decrypted C.R.F. message) or SANCTION ENFORCED. Read the report in a neutral voice, then play the epilogue (p. 4).

04 · Playing the Coordinator he knows everything

Halvorsen received the intercepted copy “for execution”. He knows who D.R. is and what he copied. He gives nothing away: he calls him “Agent” like the others, keeps his voice flat, doesn't look at him.

  • “Please proceed to the room of your choice.”
  • “Sealing in progress.”
  • “I follow company protocol.”
  • “No exemptions are provided for, Agent.”
  • He follows his paper protocol sheets, period. No favors, no vendetta.
  • If D.R. accuses him: a silence, then the next line.
  • Reading his face: D.R.'s Mental (3) vs. Halvorsen's Social (2, advantage from Discipline ■■■). Even if beaten, he shows only a fleeting unease.

05 · 3D6 Système rolls ideas

  • Mental, Average (2), to keep his composure on seeing the INTERCEPTED stamp; advantage from Meditation. Failure: −1 EP and a tremor the other agents notice.
  • Infiltration: Physical, Average (2), advantage, to approach the terminal unseen, if the Supervisor chooses that staging.
  • Investigation: Mental, Easy (1), advantage, to notice the send time (23:47): management intercepted it that same night; rank 2 (1 EP): realize his attachments are in their hands.
  • Mental, Average (2), to sense a presence behind a bulkhead, each time an adjacent room is sealed. Failure: −1 EP.
  • Meditation without NS-X III: at 3 EP or less, his FLAW gives him disadvantage on Mental; a successful Meditation test (Mental, Average) cancels it for the scene.
  • EP: 1–2 weakened, 0 he collapses and stays where he is.
  • The dice color the story; they never change the moves or the sealings.

## 3D6 SYSTÈME: roll 3D6; each die ≤ the tested attribute (Physical, Mental or Social, from 1 to 5) = 1 success · difficulty = successes required: Easy 1, Average 2, Hard 3, Extreme 4 · advantage = 4D6, disadvantage = 2D6 (all dice count; no stacking, one cancels the other) · triple (111…666): ≤ attribute = critical success, > attribute = critical failure · opposed test: most successes wins (tie: reroll, advantage to the player, or draw) · mastery: rank 1 = advantage, rank 2 = narrative effect (often 1 EP), rank 3 = major effect · EP = Physical + Mental.

06 · The briefing room B-7

Setup: the app's intro screens are the wall monitors of room B-7. The Supervisor holds the device facing the player like a monitor; the instructor (the Coordinator) stands beside it and scrolls through the screens. Rules monitors 1 to 4 show at the bottom QUESTIONS: NOT AUTHORIZED.

The player sits among three other Agents in disciplinary session, the “silhouettes”. They don't know D.R. is the target: each believes they are being punished for a minor offense.

  • Pike, the nervous one — “Disciplinary? I didn't do anything!” — “Your question is noted.”
  • Brannigan, the braggart — “This is a punishment? A stroll.” “Who ordered the session?” — “That point is not covered by procedure.”
  • Okoye, the silent one — watches D.R. a little too long. Does she know something? No: she's scared, like the others.

Use: they ask the questions the player doesn't dare to, and get shut down; the player thus gauges the wall he's up against. They give D.R. a chance to test his cover (Social, Discipline). During the game, their fate stays offscreen.

07 · The instructor dodges zero answers

  • “It's all on the screen.”
  • “Next question.”
  • “That point will be covered during the session.”
  • “The company has already answered.”
  • “Look at screen 3.”
  • “Your question is noted.”
  • “That point is not covered by procedure.”
  • “Next screen.”
  • “I am not authorized to answer.”
  • “Sit down, Agent.”

Prying out a clue: Social test, difficulty Hard (3), or Extreme (4) if the question concerns the protocol. Advantage if the player draws on a useful mastery or on a silhouette who has just pushed; disadvantage if he has already been shut down or if he interrupts the instructor.

What you get: a clue for atmosphere, never game information. For example: a brief glance at the floor, a burn scar on the hand, “Stay calm: that's all that matters.” Nothing about the sealing order or the protocol: the instructor follows his paper protocol sheets, period.

FROM Lieutenant D.R. · encrypted channel C.R.F.-7 · TO Civil Resistance Force Earth HQ · DATE 10/22/2184 · 23:47

SUBJECT Nostromo II – infiltration report · ATT. protocoles_AIP_1-4.enc, plan_NOSTROMO_II.enc

“HQ,

I have infiltrated the secret Nostromo II lunar base. Weyland-Yutani trains Psychic Agents here to sense the presence of Aliens.

Its xenomorph control project goes on, despite the failures and the deaths. Adult specimens are confined beneath the floor.

I am attaching the base map and a copy of the intrusion protocols: the order in which they seal the rooms during training. If they catch me with these documents, the company will feed me to its creatures.

I'm holding on thanks to NS-X III. Prepare the extraction.

Under the bars (for the Supervisor): in the message, “The hosts are said to be death-row convicts.”; in the header, “If he survives, procedure 7-B.” A critical failure on Investigation can make the player believe he has read everything.

09 · ENDINGS Security Directorate reports

DISCIPLINARY SESSION COMPLETE.
THE INFILTRATOR SURVIVED

“Final position: [room]. The individual “Lieutenant D.R.” survived every sealing of the protocol. Outcome not in line with Directorate expectations. ”

Decrypted C.R.F. message: “Received, Lieutenant. Extraction arranged tonight, airlock 3. Keep the protocols.”

Epilogue: night, airlock 3 corridor. The C.R.F. shuttle is there, engines off. But the black bar hid “Procedure 7-B triggered”: who is really waiting for him beyond the airlock? One last Physical (Infiltration) test, Hard (3), and the Supervisor wraps up.

SANCTION ENFORCED

“Room sealed: [room]. A specimen was introduced. The individual “Lieutenant D.R.” was inside. The disciplinary decision has been carried out. No trace will be passed to the C.R.F. File closed. ”

Epilogue: on Earth, at C.R.F. HQ, the Lieutenant's channel stays silent. Under the bar: “Stolen documents recovered from the remains.” The next day, a new trainee agent arrives aboard Nostromo II. What if he is the next infiltrator?
